    Checking the results

    Plandot does its best to find callouts and place pins, but drawings vary and results can be wrong. Check the pins before you rely on them. The calibration file in the app helps you confirm that pins land where you expect. Plandot is provided as it is, and we cannot promise it will always be available or error free.

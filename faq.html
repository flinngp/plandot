Plandot

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about your data, your drawings and what to do when something doesn't look right.

New to Plandot? Start with the how to guide.

Is my drawing uploaded anywhere?

No. Plandot runs entirely in your browser. Your drawing, your template and your rules are read on your own computer and never sent to a server. The files it makes are created on your computer too.

Your browser remembers only your rules (including the outline of any symbol you boxed), plan name, settings and language, so they are still there next time. Drawings and templates are not kept. Start over clears them, and so does clearing your browser's site data.

Does Plandot change my Fieldwire project?

No. Plandot never connects to Fieldwire. It only makes a file that you copy into the task importer yourself. Pins are added as new items with a blank ID, so nothing already in your project is edited or deleted.

Which PDFs work?

PDFs exported from design software such as AutoCAD or Revit, where the text is real text and the lines are real lines. Callouts can only be found if you can select the text in a PDF reader. A scan or a photo of a drawing has no text for Plandot to read, and it will tell you so.

Plandot can't open a DWG or another CAD file directly. Export or plot the sheet to PDF from your CAD software first, keeping the text as text rather than outlines or an image.

What if my PDF has more than one page?

You choose one page at a time. Each page is its own plan in Fieldwire, so you make one import file per page. Use Save setup to keep your rules, then Open setup on the next drawing.

Something wasn't found, or the wrong things were found.

Callouts:

  • The text must match exactly, including capital letters, and not be broken into separate pieces in the PDF.
  • Callouts drawn as outlines or part of an image can't be read. Try exporting the PDF again with text kept as text.
  • Check the search area isn't limited to part of the sheet.
  • If the rule uses a pattern, check the pattern.

Symbols: slide How closely a copy must match towards Loose to catch more copies, or towards Exact to avoid wrong ones. Tick the options for turned or mirrored copies, or for copies at a different size, if they apply. Draw the box tightly around one clean example, with nothing else inside it.

Are the pins in exactly the right place?

Pins are placed by percentage across and down the sheet, which is how Fieldwire reads the import file. They should land on the callout or the dot at the end of its leader line, but always look before you import. For a new kind of drawing, use Check pin positions first: it makes nine test pins you can import to confirm positions.

Fieldwire's task importer takes up to 500 rows at a time. If you have more pins than that, Plandot tells you how many batches to paste from the same file.

Which browsers and languages does Plandot support?

Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox. A laptop or desktop is best, because large drawings are awkward on a phone.

Thirteen languages: English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese, Danish, Swedish, Norwegian, Finnish, Polish and Czech. The translations haven't yet been checked by native speakers, so please tell us if something reads oddly. These help pages are in English only for now.

What does Plandot cost?

It's completely free!

Something went wrong? Let us know

We'd like to hear about it. When Plandot shows an error, press Copy details next to it, then paste the text into our feedback form. The details contain the version, browser and a few settings, and never a file name or anything from your drawing.

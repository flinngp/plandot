    1 Drawing

    Callouts must be real text in the PDF, not part of a scanned image.

    This page has no selectable text. It is probably a scan or a photo of a drawing, so callouts can't be found on it. Ask for the PDF exported straight from the design software. Symbols can still be found if the lines are real vector lines.

    3 Rules

    A rule turns every callout or symbol it finds into one pin.

    A setup file keeps your rules, plan name and room settings, so you can reuse them on other drawings or share them.

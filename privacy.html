Plandot

    Privacy notice

    What Plandot does with your information, in plain language. Last updated 16 October 2026.

    The short version

    • Your drawings and templates never leave your browser. Plandot reads them on your own computer and does not upload them anywhere.
    • You can try the sample without an account, and nothing about you is collected.
    • To use your own files you sign in with your email address. We keep only your email address, your plan and your saved rules.
    • You can download everything we hold about you, or delete your account, at any time from the Account box.

    Who is responsible

    Plandot is run by Flinn Ginder-Poulsen, an individual based in the United Kingdom, who is the controller of the personal information described here. Plandot is an independent tool. It is not made or endorsed by Fieldwire by Hilti.

    To contact us about your information, use the feedback form and say that it is a privacy request.

    What we collect, and why

    If you do not sign in: we count visits without cookies and without personal data, using GoatCounter. It records page views and a few named events (for example that a file was exported, and a rough range for the number of pins), never anything from a drawing. This helps us see whether Plandot is useful.

    If you sign in:

    • Your email address, so we can send you a sign-in code and recognise your account.
    • Your plan (for example free). Addresses at hilti.com and fieldwire.com are marked as staff accounts, which have free access.
    • Your saved rules: the rule names, the text you search for, pin title patterns, room settings, the plan name you entered, and the shape of any symbol you boxed. They are saved so they follow you to other computers.
    • A sign-in token kept in your browser's storage so you stay signed in. It is needed for sign-in to work, and it is removed when you sign out.

    We use this information to run your account, keep it secure, and prevent abuse. Our lawful bases under UK GDPR are performing the service you ask for when you create an account (contract) and our legitimate interest in keeping Plandot secure and working.

    We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or send marketing emails. The only emails are sign-in codes and essential messages about your account.

    Who else handles it

    • Supabase stores accounts and saved rules and handles sign-in. The data is held in a UK or EU region.
    • Resend delivers the sign-in emails, so it sees your email address and the code.
    • GitHub Pages serves the website, so it sees the usual technical details of a visit (such as your IP address).
    • GoatCounter counts visits, as above.

    These companies act for us under their own data processing terms. Some may process information outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards in those terms.

    How long we keep it

    We keep your account and saved rules until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your email address and saved rules from our database straight away. If a provider keeps backups, they expire on that provider's normal schedule. If an account is not used, we may delete it after a long period of inactivity.

    Your rights

    Under UK data protection law you can ask to see your information, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how it is used, and receive a copy you can move elsewhere.

    • See or take a copy: sign in, open Account, then choose Download my data.
    • Delete: sign in, open Account, then choose Delete my account.
    • Anything else: use the feedback form above.

    If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

    Keeping it safe

    • Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
    • There are no passwords to leak: you sign in with a code sent to your email.
    • The database only lets you read and change your own rows. Visitors who are not signed in can read nothing.
    • The website loads scripts only from itself and GoatCounter, which limits what a malicious page could do.

    No system is perfectly secure. If something goes wrong with your information, we will tell you and the regulator where the law requires it.

    Changes

    If this notice changes in a way that matters, we will say so on the sign-in screen, and the date at the top will change.

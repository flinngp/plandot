Plandot

    Privacy notice

    What Plandot does with your information, in plain language. Last updated 16 October 2026.

    The short version

    • Your drawings and templates never leave your browser. Plandot reads them on your own computer and does not upload them anywhere.
    • You can try the sample without an account, and nothing about you is collected apart from anonymous visit counts.
    • To use your own files you sign in with your email address. We keep only your email address, your plan and the rules you choose to save.
    • There are no passwords, no advertising, no tracking cookies and no selling of data.
    • You can download everything we hold about you, or delete your account, at any time from the Account box.

    Who is responsible

    Plandot is run by Flinn Ginder-Poulsen, an individual based in the United Kingdom, who is the controller of the personal information described here. Plandot is an independent tool. It is not made or endorsed by Fieldwire by Hilti.

    To contact us about your information, use the feedback form and say that it is a privacy request. We aim to reply within one month.

    What we collect, and why

    If you do not sign in: we count visits without cookies and without personal data, using GoatCounter. It records page views and a few named events (for example that a file was exported, and a rough range for the number of pins), never anything from a drawing. This helps us see whether Plandot is useful. The website host and these services may also see technical details that every website visit involves, such as your IP address.

    If you sign in:

    • Your email address, so we can send you a sign-in code and recognise your account.
    • Your plan (for example free). Addresses at hilti.com and fieldwire.com are marked as staff accounts, which have free access.
    • Your saved rules, only when you press Save rules: each rule's name, the text you search for, the pin title pattern, where the pin goes, any values you typed into a rule's other columns (such as a category or assignee), and the shape of any symbol you boxed. Please do not type personal information about other people into rules. Drawings, templates and the plan name are never saved.
    • Security records kept by our sign-in provider, such as the time and IP address of sign-in attempts, used to keep accounts safe and stop abuse.

    Stored on your own device: a sign-in token (so you stay signed in), your language choice and your current rules, kept in your browser's local storage. These are strictly necessary for Plandot to work as you asked. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. Signing out removes the sign-in token.

    We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, make automated decisions about you, or send marketing emails. The only emails are sign-in codes and essential messages about your account.

    Our lawful bases

    • Contract: to run the account you ask for and provide saved rules (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
    • Legitimate interests: to keep Plandot secure, prevent abuse and understand, without personal data, whether it is useful (Article 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time.
    • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or share information (Article 6(1)(c)).

    Who else handles it

    • Supabase stores accounts and saved rules and handles sign-in and sign-in emails. It acts for us as a processor.
    • GitHub Pages serves the website, so it sees the usual technical details of a visit (such as your IP address).
    • GoatCounter counts visits, as above.
    • Google Forms receives anything you choose to send through the feedback form. Please do not include drawings or personal information in feedback.
    • An email delivery provider may be used to send sign-in emails. If we add one, it sees your email address and the code, and we will update this notice.

    These companies act for us under their own data processing terms. Some may process information outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards in those terms, such as the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses or the UK data bridge, as applicable.

    How long we keep it

    We keep your account and saved rules until you delete them or your account. Deleting a saved rule or your account removes it from our database straight away. If a provider keeps backups, they expire on that provider's normal schedule. Security records are kept by our provider for a short period. Anonymous visit counts are kept as totals. If an account has not been used for a long time we may delete it.

    Your rights

    Under UK data protection law you can ask to see your information, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how it is used, and receive a copy you can move elsewhere. You can also withdraw from the service at any time.

    • See or take a copy: sign in, open Account, then choose Download my data.
    • Correct or delete saved rules: sign in, open Account, then choose My saved rules.
    • Delete everything: sign in, open Account, then choose Delete my account.
    • Anything else: use the feedback form above.

    If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

    Children

    Plandot is a professional tool and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has signed up, tell us and we will delete the account.

    Keeping it safe

    • Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
    • There are no passwords to leak: you sign in with a code sent to your email, and codes can be used only once and expire quickly.
    • The database lets you read and change only your own rows. Visitors who are not signed in can read nothing.
    • The website loads scripts only from itself and GoatCounter, and refuses to run inside another site's page, which limits what a malicious page could do.
    • Our sign-in provider limits repeated sign-in attempts.
    • Access to the account database is limited to the person running Plandot and protected with two-step sign-in.

    No system is perfectly secure. If something goes wrong with your information, we will tell you and the Information Commissioner's Office where the law requires it. You can report a security problem through the feedback form.

    Changes

    If this notice changes in a way that matters, we will say so on the sign-in screen, and the date at the top will change.
